
### Exercise 5. Angle Between Vectors

Given the vectors

$$
u=(1,1,0),\qquad v=(1,0,1)
$$

**1. Predicting the type of angle using the dot product**

First, calculate the dot product.

$$
\begin{aligned}
u\cdot v &= 1\cdot 1+1\cdot 0+0\cdot 1 \\
&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the dot product is positive, the angle between the vectors is acute.

**2. Computing the angle**

The angle between two nonzero vectors is calculated using the formula

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{u\cdot v}{\|u\|\|v\|}
$$

First, calculate the lengths of the vectors.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|u\| &= \sqrt{1^2+1^2+0^2} \\
&= \sqrt{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|v\| &= \sqrt{1^2+0^2+1^2} \\
&= \sqrt{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute the values into the angle formula.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos\theta &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}} \\
&= \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\theta=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=60^\circ
$$

**3. Comparing the result with the prediction**

The calculated angle is $60^\circ$, which is an acute angle because it is smaller than $90^\circ$.

This agrees with the positive dot product obtained in the first step.
